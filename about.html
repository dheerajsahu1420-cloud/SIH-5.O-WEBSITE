About SIH 5.0

Sistec innovation hakathone is a platform that encourages students think creatively and solve real-world problems using technology. It brings students, colleges, and industries together to develop new ideas and useful solutions. It also helps students improve their technical skills, teamwork, problem-solving abilities, and confidence. Through this platform, students get an opportunity to turn their ideas into real projects and make a positive impact on society.

Why Participate?

Improve your skills, work in teams and build innovative solutions. Participating in SIH 5.o students a chance to learn new technologies and show their creativity. Students can work in teams, share ideas, and solve real-world challenges. It helps them improve their coding skills, communication, and problem-solving abilities. They also get practical experience by building projects and learning from experts. It is a great opportunity to gain confidence and prepare for future careers.
