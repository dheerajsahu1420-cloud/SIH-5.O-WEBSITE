How It Works

Start your innovation journey with SIH 5.0. Follow these steps, register your team, explore challenges, and turn your creative ideas into real-world solutions.

01. Student Registration

Begin your journey by registering yourself for SIH 5.0. Fill in the required details, provide your academic information, and complete the registration process. Make sure all the information is correct before submitting your registration.

02. Build Your Team

Form a team with students who have different skills, knowledge, and interests. Each team must have a minimum of 3 members. Choose teammates who can contribute to different parts of the project and work together towards a common goal.

03. Explore Problem Statements

Explore the available problem statements and understand the challenges faced in different fields. Identify the main problem, discuss possible solutions with your team, and select a challenge that matches your interests and technical skills.

04. Plan Your Solution

After selecting a problem statement, start planning your solution. Research the topic, understand user requirements, select suitable technologies, and prepare a basic project design. Divide responsibilities among team members to manage the work effectively.

05. 18-Hour Hackathon

Get ready for an exciting 18-hour hackathon experience. Work together with your teammates, write code, develop your project, solve technical challenges, and build a working solution. Manage your time properly and focus on completing your project within the given duration.

06. Project Submission

Once your project is ready, prepare your final submission. Explain the problem, your proposed solution, technologies used, important features, and practical applications. Make sure your project details and presentation are clear and properly organized.

07. Present Your Innovation

Present your project confidently in front of the judges. Demonstrate how your solution works, explain its unique features, and highlight how it can solve real-world problems. Showcase your teamwork, creativity, and technical knowledge.
